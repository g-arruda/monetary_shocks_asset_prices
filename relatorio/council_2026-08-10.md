## Referee Recommendation

**Major Revision.** Unanimous in substance: the Harsh Referee's explicit verdict is Major Revision (reject at top-5 calibration, tractable revision for ANPEC/field); the Methodologist's four Severity-1 issues are all fixable with post-processing on artifacts already stored; the Skeptic's central objection has a decisive test whose regressor is already computed in the repo; the Macro Theorist's Finding 1 is an arithmetic correction, not a re-estimation.

The recommendation is Major rather than R&R because **three of the paper's four headline claims are currently contradicted by evidence the authors already hold** — the medium-run synchronization (contradicted by `output/factors/factor_stationarity.md`), the equity null (contradicted by `output/assets/asset_representation.md`), and the GRG reconciliation deferred to future research at `:551` (already performed in the archived referee2 replication). It is not Reject because the central *fact* — a Copom-day-identified contractionary shock is followed by BRL depreciation and simultaneous EMBI+/CDS widening, all excluding zero at 90% — survives every objection raised. What does not survive is the *mechanism attribution* built on top of it.

## Identification Verdict

**The monetary shock is plausibly identified at impact. The risk-premium mechanism is not identified at all.** These are separable, and the paper does not separate them.

**What holds.** At `h = 0` the companion matrix does not enter (`B₀ = I`), so the impact block is insulated from every dynamic-inference objection the panel raises (Methodologist, explicitly). The instrument's construction is correct in the one place applied papers usually get it wrong — Bauer-Swanson orthogonalization on strictly predetermined regressors *before* the Jarociński-Karadi sign classification, so the mask is predetermined rather than built from contemporaneous residuals (`:262`). Three critics independently call §5.2 exemplary falsification practice; that judgment is unanimous and should be defended in the response letter, not conceded.

**Three live threats to exogeneity, in descending order of severity:**

1. **External, untested.** The Skeptic found that `data/fomc_dates.csv` does not exist ✅ (verified: file absent; `script/instrument.R:95` supplies an empty vector; `build_variants.R:244` therefore sets `fomc_coincide` identically FALSE; no diagnostic consumes it). Copom met on the FOMC decision day in 7 of 8 2025 meetings and 6 of 8 in 2023; seven of the top-20 leverage days are exact same-day FOMC decisions, ≈19% of Σ|z|. A hawkish Fed surprise inside the Wed→Thu window raises DI, drops Ibovespa (**retained** by the JK filter), depreciates BRL and widens EMBI/CDS — the entire headline, no domestic channel required. The BS layer cannot remove it: its RHS is predetermined by construction, so an in-window shock passes through untouched. The paper's claim at `:519` that the surviving alternative is "um confundidor doméstico" is, as written, false.

2. **The placebo battery is mislabeled and weaker than claimed.** ✅ Verified: the panel's `sp500_vix` has mean 18.58, min 9.51, max 53.54 — that is the VIX index. `data/raw_data.csv` contains **no S&P 500 level series at all**. The paper describes this placebo at `:501`/`:509` as S&P 500 combined with VIX, and argues at `:519` that "uma surpresa fiscal brasileira também deixaria o S&P 500 parado." The S&P 500 is not in the panel. This is a straightforward factual correction and it weakens the placebo section precisely where the FOMC objection bites.

3. **Test C cleans values, not the mask.** `jk_sovereign_confound.R:621-622` — both `z_jk_bs_norisk` variants pass `jk_monetary_bs`, derived from un-orthogonalized `e_di_bs`/`e_ibov_bs` (`build_variants.R:309-314`); 60 non-zero months vs production's 62. Against the project's own fidelity-audit headline that *instrument strength lives in the mask, not the purified values*, this means the sentence at `:538` — "a magnitude do resultado central sobrevive a remover esse conteúdo **por completo**" ✅ (verified verbatim) — overstates what was done.

**On the mechanism.** Two critics reach the same verdict by different routes: the 5y CDS is a USD-denominated default spread while the UIP wedge is a BRL currency premium (Macro Theorist F2), and the measured compensation is an order of magnitude too small — 29bp CDS / 20bp EMBI against a required wedge of 3.5–4pp (Harsh Referee RR1). Neither critic can find a fiscal-dominance theory reference in `references.bib`: no Blanchard, Loyo, Sargent-Wallace, Leeper, Cochrane, Uribe. The hypothesis is sourced only to the empirical paper that rejects it (`goncalves2025`). The panel contains **no fiscal series** — no debt, no primary balance, no LFT share — and the model is linear and time-invariant, so "fiscal credibility conditions the direction of the FX response" (`:553`) ✅ is untestable within the estimated object by construction.

**Partly independent, partly transmission.** The three convergences named in the brief do not have the same epistemic status and should not be reported as though they do.

Four critics agreeing here is four critics reading the same sentence. **Two of them went beyond it and those extensions are independent evidence:** the Macro Theorist derived the consequence that the same mode makes the cumulative interest differential negative (in no repo file), and the Harsh Referee derived the inversion — that the series cited as the exception is the only non-mechanical one, and that the mode also drives credit and `juros_selic`, making it general rather than risk-specific (also in no repo file).

Neither is in any repository document. That half is independent.
- **The GRG convergence is pure transmission.** Both critics who raise it cite the same archived CSV and the same `stopifnot` line. Two readings of one file is one piece of evidence, not two.

### D1 — Is the impact block insulated, or is it inherently a present-value object?

- **Methodologist:** at h = 0, B₀ = I and Â does not enter, so "the impact results (h ≤ 5) — the title claim — are largely insulated" from every dynamic-inference objection. The defensible core.
- **Macro Theorist:** UIP is a present-value relation. The impact exchange rate *is* a function of the whole expected path, so the impact number cannot be interpreted without the medium-run path — and that path, computed from the paper's own file, contributes depreciation via the differential and appreciation via the risk premium, reversing the abstract's sign attribution.
- **Disputed premise:** whether the h = 0 FX response is a self-contained estimate (Methodologist) or an object whose *interpretation* requires exactly the horizons the Methodologist just showed are inferentially unreliable (Macro Theorist).
- **Both can be right, and that is the problem for the paper.** The impact estimate can be a clean fact while the UIP interpretation of it is unavailable — because the interpretation needs h = 12-48 responses whose bands, per R2(d), are not valid at T = 147 with a dominant root of 0.977.
- **Resolves by:** the Macro Theorist's decomposition table at T ∈ {6, 12, 24, 48} with bootstrap bands. If the wedge is large and stable across truncations and the risk contribution stays small, the paper reports a *fact* (depreciation + widening) and abandons the *dominance* claim — Methodologist's core survives, Macro Theorist's objection stands. If the wedge is truncation-sensitive, the title must go. Post-processing only.

Back-of-envelope: domestic 6-month carry gain ~25bp; BRL jumps 3.64% the wrong way and reverts by h≈10, implying a required shift in expected excess return on the order of 3.5-4pp. Measured sovereign compensation is 29bp/20bp — an order of magnitude too small. Strongest rival: currency risk premium (Dalgıç-Özhan, cited twice then dropped) rather than sovereign credit risk premium. Decisive diagnostic: add the CIP basis/cupom cambial to the panel; if it moves on the order of the FX jump, reframe around Dalgıç-Özhan; if flat, the paper is entitled only to "shock depreciates BRL and widens spreads," a fact not a channel.

**2. The medium-run "synchronized reversal" is one eigenvalue, and the authors' evidence is inverted relative to what they claim.** The tell: `:369` says FX does NOT follow the reversal, and `factor_stationarity.md:160` shows cambio_usd is the ONE series whose reversal survives deletion of the dominant pair. The paper's evidence is exactly inverted: the series cited as synchronized are the mechanical ones; the series cited as the exception has the only independent reversal. The dominant mode also produces the medium-run trough in all six credit aggregates and in juros_selic — a general persistence mode, not a risk-premium mode.

**3. The equity null is a panel-representation artifact reported as a finding.** Under level representation, equity block goes from 0 to 28 sig90 cells, 7/8 indices significant h=0-3, ξ_mp barely moves (10.23 vs 10.43). The paper's own diagnostic file labels this "MECANICO CONFIRMADO." h=0 is a clean test (cumsum is a no-op there) — point estimate roughly doubles, not just the band, ruling out a spurious-precision rival.

**4. §6 does not adjudicate against Gonçalves et al. (2025), and the authors already have the adjudication.** "Frequency or identification" is a disjunction presented as resolution while resolving neither. The daily replication converts it into a result: disagreement is frequency/propagation, not identification. Must report with its own diagnostics and the inconvenient IBOV cell (wrong sign, unidentified) alongside the favorable BRL cell, or it's cherry-picking.

### Important But Not Fatal

ξ_mp=10.43 is the selection criterion, marginal, and AR intervals are named but never computed; the identified shock barely moves Selic itself (h=0 −4.7bp vs +50bp normalization), and the paper calls Selic both a "negative control" and evidence the policy rate "accompanies" the reversal — contradictory uses; the two-tier reading rule is applied correctly in §4 but abandoned in abstract/conclusion; cross-paper magnitude comparisons at mismatched shock sizes (3.64%/50bp vs GRG's per-100bp figures — rescaled this paper is ~7.3%/100bp); the small-VAR benchmark exists in the repo but not in the paper, and the DFM never wins on precision; yield curve provenance undocumented.

### Strengths Worth Preserving

§5.2's pre-registered rule and honest reporting of inconvenient numbers; the orthogonalize-to-risk result (ξ_mp 10.43→12.68, FX unchanged) is the paper's most persuasive number and is buried; the Selic negative-control construction; combining BS+JK is a genuine, under-claimed methodological contribution.

### Open Pass

The paper is titled for the result it can least defend and framed away from the result it can most defend. "UIP Inverted" requires an expectations measure the paper doesn't have. The most robust, most novel object is nearly a footnote: the shock moves the 5y DI vertex 1.85× the 6-month vertex it's normalized on, with the overnight rate not moving on impact at all — a term-premium amplification result, exactly what a large-panel DFM is built to detect, exactly what makes a rigorous Brazil-vs-advanced-economy comparison possible. Reframing around this makes the FX inversion a corollary rather than the load-bearing claim.
